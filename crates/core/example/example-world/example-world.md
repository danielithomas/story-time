# Example World

This is an example: a world with one book, made to show where Story Time keeps a book and everything around it. Look around and change anything. When you're done, delete the `example-world` folder from your bookshelf; nothing else depends on it.

A world holds the books set in it, and the world bible they share. A book you start gets a world of its own, named after it. Name the world yourself when more books may be set in it.

## Books

- [[a-tour-of-story-time|A Tour of Story Time]]

## World bible

- `bible/characters/`: who they are, how they look and speak, what drives them
- `bible/locations/`: where things happen
- `bible/world/`: how the world works, and what it costs
- [[example-world-timeline|Example World timeline]]: what happened when, including what no scene shows
