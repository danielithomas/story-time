A scrap is a fragment kept for later: a line of dialogue, an image, a scene you cut. This one belongs to the example's world, but not yet to a book. Scraps are kept exactly as they arrived, and their words count towards your day but not towards any book. When one finds its place, the inspector can make it a scene in a book, or note the scene it led to.
