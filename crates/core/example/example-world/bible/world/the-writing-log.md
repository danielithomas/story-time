# The Writing Log

## What it is

Story Time's record of how much you write. As you work, it notes each book's and each scratchpad's word count in `.story-time/history.csv`.

## How it works

Words written in a day are the net change across your whole bookshelf, so deleting words counts against the day, and moving a scene into a scratchpad doesn't count at all. The status bar shows today's words against your daily goal, and your streak.

## Rules and limits

This example's words were logged when it was added, so they don't count as words you wrote that day.

## Who it affects

The writer, every day.
