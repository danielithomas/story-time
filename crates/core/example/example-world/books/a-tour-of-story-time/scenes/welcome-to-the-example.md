# Welcome to the Example

Everything you write in Story Time lives on your bookshelf: a folder of plain Markdown files that other editors can open too. Each world on it has a folder, and each book in a world has a folder of its own.

You're reading a scene. The binder on the left lists this book's contents, part by part and chapter by chapter, with each scene's status and word count. Click a scene to open it. Below the contents come the world bible, then the world's research and scratchpad, then the general ones that belong to no world, with your ideas.

The Writer, whose point of view every scene here takes, has a character note in the bible, and The Binder has a location note. Open them to see what goes in each.

When you've seen enough, delete the example-world folder from your bookshelf. Nothing else depends on it.
