# A Scene Set Aside

This scene isn't in the book's contents, so the binder lists it under Unplaced. It still belongs to the book, and its words count, but it isn't exported until you place it.

Unplaced is for a scene you've written but can't place yet, or one you've taken out and might put back. A scene you've cut for good can go to the scratchpad instead, where its words still count towards your day but not towards any book.
