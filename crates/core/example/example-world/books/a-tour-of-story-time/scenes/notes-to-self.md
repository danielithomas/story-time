# Notes to Self

A draft is allowed to be rough. While you write, leave yourself notes the reader will never see. %% Like this one. Anything between two pairs of percent signs is a note to self. %%

A note to self is dimmed in the editor, isn't counted in your words, and is left out when you export the book. A callout works the same way, and stands out more:

> [!todo]
> Check this paragraph once the next scene is written.

Notes like these let you keep drafting without stopping to fix things. The status in the inspector says how far along a scene is: outline, draft, revised or final. This one is a draft.
